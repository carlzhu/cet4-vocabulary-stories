## Chapter continuity contract

Every chapter record in `curriculum_plan.csv` must specify its arc, immediate plot beat, new target IDs, contextual review IDs, estimated word count, and page estimate. A later chapter may revise an earlier belief but may not erase an established event.

## Review time model

- Learning event: chapter/day 0.
- Planned reviews: approximately +1, +3, +7, +14, and +30 chapters/days.
- Reviews beyond Chapter 137 remain post-course obligations rather than being silently dropped.
- Only a capacity-limited subset is scheduled as contextual story reuse; other due reviews use cloze, collocation, or active recall.
